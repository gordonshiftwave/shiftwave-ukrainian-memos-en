Ukrainian Rescue Truck Drivers

In 2022, volunteer drivers in Ukraine drove day and night to carry civilians out of areas under attack, often with little sleep and under constant danger. Pavlo is one of them. According to Mike North, Ph.D., Shiftwave Co-Founder, Inventor and CEO, who filmed this video in April 2022, the drivers had evacuated 9,000 kids so far. Here is what Pavlo said right after a Shiftwave session.
