Ukrainian Rescue Truck Drivers

On February 24, 2022, Russia launched its full-scale invasion of Ukraine, and millions of people fled toward the borders with Poland and other neighboring countries. Volunteer drivers made run after run, often for days on end, carrying women, children, and families out of danger. Pablo is one of those drivers, filmed in April 2022 right after a Shiftwave session.
